Lead cell therapy

Cenplacel-L: placental cells, now available to Florida physicians

Cenplacel-L is Celularity's most mature cell therapy: placenta-derived mesenchymal-like adherent stromal cells (MLASCs), developed over more than two decades of research by Celularity and its predecessor, Celgene Cellular Therapeutics.

Florida Statutes § 458.3245 and § 459.0127, effective July 1, 2025, allow licensed physicians to perform certain cell therapies not yet approved by the FDA, under strict sourcing, manufacturing, accreditation and consent requirements. On June 4, 2026 Celularity and Fountain Life announced that cenplacel-L is available for investigational use by licensed physicians in the state, with Fountain Life's longevity-focused physician network as the first clinical channel. Fountain Life's founding partners include Celularity's CEO, Dr. Robert Hariri, and director Dr. Peter H. Diamandis.
